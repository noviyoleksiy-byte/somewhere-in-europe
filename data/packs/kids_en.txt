#1 [DK]
This country is where the world-famous colorful plastic building bricks LEGO were invented.
+++
Fairy tale writer Hans Christian Andersen, creator of "The Little Mermaid" and "The Snow Queen", also lived here.
---
Denmark — Copenhagen. Millions of families visit the original LEGOLAND park in Billund.

#2 [SE]
In the capital of this country lives the playful rooftop character Karlsson, and Pippi Longstocking was born in books written here.
---
Sweden — Stockholm. Author Astrid Lindgren created some of the most beloved children's heroes ever written.

#3 [FI]
In a cozy forest valley of this land of lakes live the gentle white Moomins, drawn by Tove Jansson.
---
Finland — Helsinki. You can visit a real Moominworld island theme park in Naantali.

#4 [IT]
This country is home to the classic story of Pinocchio, the wooden puppet whose nose grew whenever he told a fib.
+++
Kids everywhere also adore its two famous foods: pizza and creamy gelato.
---
Italy — Rome. Carlo Collodi wrote Pinocchio's adventures in Florence in the 19th century.

#5 [GB]
Young wizard Harry Potter boarded his magic train from Platform 9¾ in the capital of this island country.
+++
Red double-decker buses and the famous clock tower Big Ben stand right in the city center.
---
United Kingdom — London. King's Cross station even has a magical luggage trolley half-inside the brick wall!

#6 [FR]
A tall iron lattice tower rises above the capital of this country, and nearby kids explore Disneyland Paris.
---
France — Paris. It is also the homeland of the beloved storybook "The Little Prince".

#7 [UA]
Engineers in this country built the giant cargo plane "Mriya" ("Dream"), and its flag mirrors blue skies over golden wheat fields.
+++
Its capital also holds the deepest metro station in the world — Arsenalna.
---
Ukraine — Kyiv. Arsenalna station sits 105.5 meters deep beneath the city streets.

#8 [DE]
The Brothers Grimm collected timeless fairy tales like Cinderella, Little Red Riding Hood, and Snow White in this country.
---
Germany — Berlin. Fairytale castles in Bavaria even inspired animated movie palaces.

#9 [NL]
Every spring, fields here turn into giant rainbows of blooming tulips beside spinning wooden windmills.
+++
Cities here have more bicycles than people and hundreds of bridges over canals.
---
Netherlands — Amsterdam. Keukenhof gardens bloom with over seven million flower bulbs each spring.

#10 [CH]
This Alpine country is famous for snowy mountain peaks, creamy milk chocolate, and cuckoo clocks.
---
Switzerland — Bern. Gentle giant St. Bernard rescue dogs also hail from its snowy mountain passes.

#11 [NO]
Deep sea inlets called fjords slice into the coast of this northern land, where green northern lights dance in winter skies.
---
Norway — Oslo. Traditional folk tales here are packed with silly mountain trolls!

#12 [BE]
This small country gave the world the tiny blue Smurfs and warm crispy waffles!
---
Belgium — Brussels. Brussels even has a Comic Strip Museum celebrating Smurfs and Tintin.

#13 [GR]
Ancient myths tell of gods living atop Mount Olympus in this sunny land, while the white marble Parthenon crowns its capital.
---
Greece — Athens. The very first Olympic Games were held here almost 3,000 years ago.

#14 [ES]
In this warm country, architect Antoni Gaudí built a cathedral in Barcelona that looks like a giant sandcastle.
---
Spain — Madrid. It is also the homeland of funny knight Don Quixote, who mistook windmills for giants.

#15 [PT]
Sitting on the western edge of Europe by the Atlantic Ocean, this country's sailors explored the seas in wooden caravels.
---
Portugal — Lisbon. Bright yellow historic trams still rattle up and down Lisbon's steep hills.

#16 [IE]
Known as the Emerald Isle for its lush green fields, this island country uses the three-leaf shamrock as its emblem.
+++
Legends say tiny red-bearded leprechauns hide pots of gold at the end of rainbows here.
---
Ireland — Dublin. Green is worn proudly every year on St. Patrick's Day.

#17 [IS]
On this far-north island, boiling water shoots into the sky from natural geysers — and there are zero mosquitoes!
---
Iceland — Reykjavik. Volcanic heat warms outdoor swimming pools even when snow covers the ground.

#18 [PL]
The emblem of this country's capital is a brave mermaid with a sword and shield, while historic Kraków tells of the Wawel Dragon.
---
Poland — Warsaw. A metal dragon statue below Wawel Castle actually breathes real fire every few minutes!

#19 [AT]
Musical genius Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart was born in this country and started composing songs when he was just five years old.
---
Austria — Vienna. Its capital also boasts one of the oldest giant Ferris wheels in the world.

#20 [CZ]
Animators in this country created the famous Little Mole cartoon, and its capital features an ancient clock with moving figures.
---
Czechia — Prague. The Prague Astronomical Clock has ticked on the Old Town Hall for over 600 years.

#21 [RO]
High in the Carpathian mountains of Transylvania sits a dramatic hilltop castle tied to spooky legends of Count Dracula.
---
Romania — Bucharest. Real forests here are home to Europe's largest population of wild brown bears.

#22 [HR]
The famous black-and-white spotted dogs from the movie "101 Dalmatians" take their name from a sunny coastal region in this country.
+++
More than a thousand islands line its clear blue Adriatic sea.
---
Croatia — Zagreb. The region of Dalmatia gave these playful spotted dogs their famous name.
